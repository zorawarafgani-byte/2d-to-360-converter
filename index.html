About This Tool

Welcome to 3DGen AI, a lightweight utility tool designed to convert standard 2D flat photographs into interactive, full 360-degree 3D digital objects.

Powered by advanced AI neural networks and WebGL rendering technology, our platform enables creators, designers, and developers to generate 3D assets fast without specialized modeling software or experience.

How it works

  1. Paste your Tripo3D API key.
  2. Upload a clear image of a single object (PNG, JPG or WEBP, up to 5MB).
  3. Click Generate 3D Model and wait 30 to 60 seconds.
  4. Rotate the model in the viewer and download the .glb file.

Tips for better results

  • Use one object on a plain background.
  • Choose a well-lit, sharp image with the whole object visible.
  • Avoid heavy shadows, glass and very thin parts.

GLB files open in Blender, Unity, Unreal Engine, AR viewers and most web 3D tools.
